International (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution is required: it is given here, in assets/LICENSES.md and on the
title screen's credits. Each model was modified for the game: decimated,
turned to face +X and scaled to real size, materials merged, wheels split
into wheel_fl/fr/rl/rr, textures re-encoded to 1024 px JPEG (the van's badge
